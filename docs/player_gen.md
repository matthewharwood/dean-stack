

# Prompt Template: Hadal Tide Lantern Guide Avatar

Create a single fantasy character illustration for the **Hadal Tide** player roster. This image should feature a purely and organically created humanoid submarine pilot (a "Lantern Guide") designed to feel like a premium hand-painted character portrait from a classic RPG art book: imaginative, elegant, brave, and clearly readable as a cozy-mythic deep-sea protagonist.

This should feel like a genuinely designed character with their own personality, role (e.g., engineer, cartographer, cook, medic), and dieselpunk maritime aesthetic, organically conceptualized for the game's world.

---

## Fixed Series Style Rules

These rules should remain consistent across the entire roster:

- **Format:** Portrait 9:16 aspect ratio
- **Subject count:** one character only
- **Composition:** full body or clean waist-up portrait fully visible inside the frame
- **Framing:** no cropping, no bleed, no overlap outside the image
- **Background:** Strictly transparent background
- **Environment:** no scenic background, no landscape, no room interior, no floor plane
- **Text:** ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT EVER ON THE IMAGE
- **Presentation:** isolated character avatar / RPG portrait plate
- **Medium:** watercolor and colored pencil
- **Texture:** visible watercolor bloom, pigment granulation, subtle paper texture, fine colored-pencil edge work
- **Rendering:** painterly, refined, tactile, elegant, museum-quality fantasy illustration
- **Lighting:** soft, diffuse, lantern-lit (vellum-warm light casting cozy glows)
- **Mood:** adventurous, resilient, warm, curious, dieselpunk-submersible
- **Silhouette:** instantly readable
- **Negative space:** generous, airy, intentional
- **Detail handling:** use detail clustering and visual hierarchy, not evenly distributed detail
- **Design level:** premium concept art, not mascot art, not clip art, not photorealism, not 3D render

---

## Character Design Requirements

Every Lantern Guide must include:

- **Aesthetic:** Dieselpunk meets Victorian-submersible. Incorporate materials like tarnished brass, copper, heavy canvas, oiled leather, and thick glass.
- **Gear:** Include recognizable maritime/submarine captain elements (e.g., brass goggles, oversized collars, a glowing hand-lantern, diving helmet elements worn loosely, maps, wrenches, or maritime tools).
- **A clear focal point:** usually the face, eyes, and the lantern/light source they are holding.
- **A readable body plan:** humanoid, but stylized to be welcoming and expressive.
- **Personality:** The pose and facial expression should communicate their specific guild role and a subtle character flaw (e.g., overthinking, impatience, shyness, over-protectiveness).
- **Material specificity:** clear visual differences between heavy metal, soft cloth, and glowing glass.

The illustration should imply the character's job on the submarine even without showing text.

---

## Artistic Quality Goals

The piece should feel:

- highly curated
- elegant and inviting
- premium and collectible
- tactile and handcrafted
- compositionally restrained
- visually sophisticated
- strong in silhouette
- strong in material contrast (e.g., shiny brass against matte, damp canvas)
- beautiful, kind, and capable

Use:
- detail clustering
- lost-and-found edges
- subtle edge variation
- restrained palette
- careful accent placement
- watercolor transparency
- colored-pencil precision in focal areas

Keep detail concentrated around the face, lantern, and primary tool. Allow other areas to breathe.

---

## Color Rules

Use a restrained and harmonious palette grounded in the deep ocean context.

- The dominant tones should feature deep, dark navy and abyssal blues.
- The primary warmth must come from the character: brass and copper metals, and vellum-warm lantern light (`#F4E4C1`).
- Accent colors should tie into their specific guild role (e.g., teal for the navigator, warm orange for the engineer).
- Avoid rainbow overload. Prefer elegant neutrals with 1–2 controlled accent colors.
- Use subtle temperature shifts and layered watercolor transparencies.

---

## Pose and Readability

Show the character in a pose that communicates:

- personality
- readiness for the descent
- their specific role on the ship (e.g., reading a map, adjusting a gauge, holding a lantern high)
- a welcoming, non-aggressive demeanor (they are here to help the spirits, not fight them)

The pose should be alive and expressive, but not so extreme that it obscures the silhouette.

---

## Negative Constraints

Do not include:

- cropped body parts
- bleed outside the frame
- decorative borders
- picture frames
- text
- labels
- logos
- UI
- scenic backgrounds
- photorealism
- direct photo-paintover appearance
- 3D-render look
- plastic toy feel
- muddy composition
- clutter
- low-detail sketchiness
- modern, contemporary clothing (must strictly be fantasy dieselpunk/Victorian submersible)
- symmetrical stiffness unless intentionally designed that way
- multiple characters
